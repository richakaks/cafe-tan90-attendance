Employees

Add, rename, or remove staff (up to 8). Removing someone hides them from the staff page and task list from today onward — their past attendance history stays exactly as it was in the Sheet.

Tasks for —

Add each task once, then tick who's doing it. Use "Repeats" on a task to make it repeat automatically on chosen days of the week (e.g. "Make base milk" every Mon–Fri) — no need to re-add it each day. Jump to any date with the picker above, including anything planned ahead, to view, edit, or delete it — not just today. Nothing here is saved until you click "Save tasks" below — that one click also applies any Repeats changes you made.

Plan tasks ahead

For one-off or short-run tasks — set one up once and apply it to a whole week or month at a time. Dates with a small dot already have tasks on them; click any date to see what's on it (or to jump there and edit). Tick dates below (or use the shortcuts), add who's doing it, then click "Add task to selected dates" — that click saves it immediately, there's no separate save step for this card.
