Tasks for -

Click + Add task below to add a row, type its name, and tick who's doing it. Each row has a small Repeats button next to its name, it says "One-time" until you set it. Click it and tick the days of the week you want that task to happen on, for example Mon-Fri for "Make base milk", then it shows up on those days automatically, with no need to re-add it. Jump to any date with the picker above, including anything planned ahead, to view, edit, or delete it, not just today. Nothing here is saved until you click "Save tasks" below. That one click also applies any Repeats changes you made.

Plan tasks ahead

For one-off or short-run tasks: set one up once and apply it to a whole week or month at a time. Dates with a small dot already have tasks on them; click any date to see what's on it (or to jump there and edit). Tick dates below, add who's doing it, then click "Add task to selected dates". That click saves it immediately, there's no separate save step for this card.
